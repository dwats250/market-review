## 5. What is the truth today?

The driver was rates against equities again: ISM services prices jumped to 74.0 and the 10Y made a new 2002 high near 5.31–5.35% (bear steepening, 2Y flat), yet stocks rose. Capital went to megacap tech (Nasdaq record, MSFT/META/NVDA) and, less, to small caps (IWM +0.66%); defensives and real estate lagged (XLRE −0.34%). Mixed on breadth: concentrated at the top, but IWM kept pace and RSP is unknown. The unresolved tension is the same as Friday's: equities keep pricing growth while the long end prices inflation; that fits H1's yield level but not its "weighs on equities" half, and fits H4 (VIX 15.52) and H5. What would change the read: the 10Y holding above 5.35% into Wednesday's FOMC minutes with QQQ losing its lead, or RSP beating SPY on a down day.
