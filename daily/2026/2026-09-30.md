## 4. Connecting the dots (Dustin)

## 5. What is the truth today?

The driver was rates, not inflation. Soft core PCE bought a morning rally, but the growth data (GDP revised to 2.2%, ADP +90k) put yields back in charge: the 10Y rose to 5.29% (a 2002 high) and the 30Y to 5.64%, and the curve steepened to 41 bp. Money went to megacap tech/software (QQQ +0.25%) and out of nearly everything else: XLF −1.13%, IWM −0.40%, the Dow −0.86%, and 2 of 11 sectors up. Leadership was concentrated, not broad. VIX 16.34 stayed calm. That calm is the unresolved tension: a 10Y at 24-year highs, a weak average stock, and little hedging demand. This fits H1, H3, H4 and H5 on one day. What would change the read: yields falling while breadth widens (financials and small caps up with QQQ), or a VIX jump above about 20 as yields climb.
