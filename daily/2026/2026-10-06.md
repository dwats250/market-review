## 5. What is the truth today?

The driver was mixed: a company-specific catalyst (Google's 20-year nuclear deal with Constellation) plus a modest rates relief (10Y −3.6 bp from a 2002 high, 2Y −4.2 bp). Capital went to utilities (XLU +2.98%), real estate (+1.06%) and semis at records; small caps fell (IWM −0.72%). Breadth was middling: RSP +0.57% matched SPY +0.55%, but IWM's drop and XLF/XLB lagging argue against a broad catch-up. The AI-power theme is still the organizing trade: the "defensive" leadership was AI capex reaching utilities, not rotation away from tech. That fits H5 in spirit, sits awkwardly with H1 (yields easing, not weighing), and H4 holds (VIX 15.01). Unresolved: whether the 10Y's retreat is a pause or a turn ahead of CPI on Oct 14. What would change the read: XLU holding gains ex-CEG, or IWM and XLF leading on a further drop in yields.
