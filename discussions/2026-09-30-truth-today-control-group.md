# Discussion — What is the truth today?

Date: 2026-09-30
Status: context, not authority
Source: ChatGPT discussion with Dustin, committed at the owner's direction.

The journal should do more than compare Market Brief with outside sources and catalogue misses. After reviewing Market Brief, actual market behavior, the outside control group, and important missing information, state a short provisional read answering:

"What is the truth today?"

This is deliberately falsifiable and temporary. It should say what appears to be driving the market, where capital is going, what is broad versus concentrated, what tensions remain unresolved, and what evidence would change the read. Do not force a thesis when evidence is genuinely mixed.

Outside sources are a control group, not an authority. Compare coverage as: CAPTURED / PARTIAL / ABSENT / UNIQUE. The purpose is coverage differential, not ranking publications or copying their editorial priorities.

Current control-group shape:
- Macroglide: calendar, actual / consensus / prior
- Reuters: global macro and cross-asset narrative
- WSJ Markets: rates, Fed, market mechanics
- MarketWatch: breadth, sectors, internals
- Seeking Alpha Wall Street Breakfast: broad headline/corporate scanner

Other sources such as Bloomberg, FT or CNBC can be specialist/tie-breaker sources when useful.

For roughly the next two weeks, deliberately observe before designing fixes. For each meaningful gap record:
- frequency;
- whether it materially changed an interpretation;
- where in the cadence it would have helped;
- exact missing data;
- page vs analyst-context need;
- signal/noise cost;
- provenance/licensing constraints;
- implementation blast radius.

Only after that observation period ask what the smallest elegant fix is, what existing machinery it can reuse, what failure modes it creates, and what should remain unbuilt.

RSP is the equal-weight S&P 500 ETF. RSP vs SPY is a low-complexity candidate for observing concentration: SPY outperforming RSP suggests cap-weighted concentration; RSP keeping pace/outperforming suggests broader participation. Observation candidate only, not approved Market Brief work.

A separate news-scanning project may eventually complement Market Brief. Do not inject one into Market Brief as a kneejerk response to current gaps.
