## Low-value lab outputs (observations about the experiment, not removal instructions)

- **0DTE move / straddle ratio.** SPY 95% → 104% → 154% → 400%; QQQ 105% → 130% → 233% → 1087%.
  - The denominator is the nearest straddle. That is the same-day expiry, whose premium decays toward zero as the session runs out (SPY $3.35 → $0.88).
  - The numerator is the move since the prior close, which accumulates over the whole day.
  - So the ratio rises mechanically through the session, whatever the market does. Only the premarket value compares a full-day premium with a move.
- **Repeated OI-change list.** It was identical in all four snapshots: open interest is published once a day, so the open, midday and close snapshots reprint the premarket comparison.
  - Its largest entry, Oct 8 739P (+33.4k), sits about 4.5% below spot and expired that day, so "near-spot" overstates it.
  - The one pattern worth noting was there premarket: Oct 23 745P −33.1k against Oct 30 765P +25.3k and 740P +25.0k. It may be a put roll; that is not verified.
- **UNEXPLAINED at the close** listed XLE, RTX and LMT. The lab had collected oil and Hormuz headlines, but under USO, and catalyst mapping keys on ticker, not theme.
  - The flag did surface real unknowns that no source here explains: LLY −4.0%, XLV −1.84% and PFE −1.71% at midday, XLP +2.07% at the close, and VST −6.25% and AVGO −4.22% at the close.
